Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

Who processes your data

MyWorld+ is provided by Danilo Savić pr Savage Digital, Subotička 100A, 21000 Novi Sad, Serbia, company reg. no. 67712048, tax ID 114608761 ("we"). For any privacy question, write to savage.dgtl@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and the name you enter. We don't ask for your date of birth, address or phone number.
  • Content you add: documents, photos, and details of items, vehicles, reminders and family members.
  • Subscription: subscription status (plan, expiry date). Card details are handled by Google Play; we never see them.
  • Device: a notification token and, on the free plan, an advertising identifier.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, without the content of your documents.

Why we use it

  • to run the app: storage, search, family sharing, reminders;
  • for AI features (Pro plans only, and only when you start them);
  • to bill your subscription;
  • for ads on the free plan, only with your consent.

We don't sell your data and never use the content of your documents for ads. No document data is included in ad requests.

Who we share it with

  • Google Cloud / Firebase — storage, sign-in, notifications (servers in the EU and US);
  • OpenAI — AI features on Pro plans only; requests are not kept for model training;
  • RevenueCat — subscription status;
  • Google AdMob — ads, free plan only and only with consent;
  • Google Play — billing.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. Deleted documents stay in the trash for 30 days, then are permanently erased. A data export (ZIP) is available for 7 days. When you delete your account we delete your records and files; anonymised cost and subscription records are kept only as long as the law requires.

Your rights

In the app you can export all your data (Settings → Export data) and delete your account (Settings → Delete account). For correction, restriction of processing or objections, write to savage.dgtl@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, or to the data protection authority in your country.

How to delete your account: myworld-b0330.web.app/delete-account.

Children

The app is not intended for children. Child profiles added by a parent are not accounts and are managed by the parent.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by database rules so each user sees only their own data and what a family member has shared with them.
