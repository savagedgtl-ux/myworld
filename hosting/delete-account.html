Brisanje MyWorld+ naloga

Danilo Savić pr Savage Digital, Novi Sad

U aplikaciji

  1. Otvori MyWorld+ i prijavi se.
  2. Idi na Podešavanja → Obriši nalog.
  3. Potvrdi prijavom i dugmetom „Obriši”.

Bez aplikacije

Pošalji email sa adrese kojom si registrovan na savage.dgtl@gmail.com, naslov „Brisanje naloga”. Nalog brišemo u roku od 30 dana i javljamo ti kada je gotovo.

Šta se briše

Profil, dokumenti i svi fajlovi, stvari, vozila, podsetnici, uređaji i pozivnice. Ako si vlasnik porodice, članovi gube pristup deljenom sadržaju.

Šta ostaje

Anonimizovani zapisi o troškovima AI funkcija i o pretplatama, bez veze sa tvojim imenom ili emailom, koliko to zahtevaju računovodstveni propisi. Aktivnu pretplatu otkaži u Google Play-u — brisanje naloga je ne otkazuje automatski.

Delete your MyWorld+ account

In the app

  1. Open MyWorld+ and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm by signing in again and tapping “Delete”.

Without the app

Email savage.dgtl@gmail.com from the address you registered with, subject “Delete account”. We delete the account within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

What is deleted

Your profile, documents and all files, items, vehicles, reminders, devices and invitations. If you own a family, its members lose access to shared content.

What is kept

Anonymised records of AI costs and subscriptions, with no link to your name or email, only as long as accounting law requires. Cancel an active subscription in Google Play — deleting your account does not cancel it automatically.
